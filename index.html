No. 01

Alloy: Delvesmith

Every element reacts.

A top-down pixel-art action RPG. Each hero binds two of six elements, and every pair reacts both ways: fire on nature sets off Combust, frost then earth Shatters. You build your moves at the Anvil as chains of up to five, each with its own form, elements and rune sockets.

No. 02

Unhappy Campers

You forgot the tent stakes. The raccoons did not forget your truck keys.

A 2–4 player physics co-op camping trip that goes wrong in three acts. Pack late, camp badly, then hike out in the dark after the raccoons take the truck. Squirrels are thieves, bears are real danger, and something else owns the night. One run takes about an hour.

The studio

Tooth & Scale

Tooth and Scale is an independent studio. Right now it is one person, a stack of design docs, and a few test bots that play the games around the clock so the balance holds up before real players arrive.

We like systems that combine in ways nobody planned, and failures that are funny instead of punishing. Each game gets its own site once it has something to show. Start with the entries above.
